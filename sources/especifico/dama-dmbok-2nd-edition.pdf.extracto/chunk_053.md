Figure 12 Context Diagram: Data Handling Ethics 
Definition:  Data handling ethics are concerned with how to procure, store, manage, 
interpret, analyze / apply and dispose of data in ways that are aligned with ethical principles, 
including community responsibility.
Goals: 
1. To define ethical handling of data in the organization.
2. To educate staff on the organization risks of improper data handling.
3. To change/instill preferred culture and behaviors on handling data.
4. To monitor regulatory environment, measure, monitor, and adjust organization approaches for ethics in 
data.
