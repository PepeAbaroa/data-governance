1.3.3 Data Governance Operating Model Types 
In a centralized model, one Data Governance organization oversees all activities in all subject areas. In a replicated 
model, the same DG operating model and standards are adopted by each business unit. In a federated model, one 
Data Governance organization coordinates with multiple Business Units to maintain consistent definitions and 
standards. (See Figure 17 and Chapter 16.)
