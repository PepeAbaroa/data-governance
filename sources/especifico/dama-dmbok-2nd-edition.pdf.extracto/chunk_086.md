2.7 Develop Goals, Principles, and Policies 
Development of goals, principles, and policies derived from the Data Governance Strategy will guide the 
organization into the desired future state.  
Goals, principles, and policies are typically drafted by either by data management professionals, business policy 
staff, or a combination, under the auspices of data governance. Next, Data Stewards and management review and 
refine them. Then, the Data Governance Council (or similar body) conducts the final review, revision, and 
adoption.
