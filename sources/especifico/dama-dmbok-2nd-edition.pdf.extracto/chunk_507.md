1.3.5 Sources of Big Data 
Because so much of human activity is executed electronically, massive amounts of data are accumulating every 
day as we move through the world, interact with each other, and transact business. Big Data is produced 
through email, social media, online orders, and even online video games. Data is generated not only by phones 
and point-of-sale devices, but also by surveillance systems, sensors in transportation systems, medical 
monitoring systems, industrial and utility monitoring systems, satellites, and military equipment. For example, 
one airline flight can generate a terabyte of data. Devices that interact directly with the Internet generate a large 
portion of Big Data. The connections between devices and the Internet are sometimes called the Internet of 
Things (IoT).
